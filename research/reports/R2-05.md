### 2. Why it is growing

| Driver | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Weak yen | Only indirect: inbound foreign-visitor sales at Book Off rose 31.2%, and Japan's 2025 exports were boosted by the yen (generic trade news, snippet). No one has isolated the yen effect on used-goods volumes. |
| Perceived quality | Hard Off cites "high quality, cost performance, variety, rarity". A watch guide cites a real 30–50% discount against Western prices, but only after landed costs. |
| Anime, collectibles | Bunjang: K-pop about 70% of transactions. Kream: Japan trading-card online sales +218%. Book Off USA: hobby, anime and trading cards are the growth lines. Mercari's Taiwan/HK launch stresses Entertainment & Hobby. |
| Luxury | Book Off: watches, metals and luxury are 33% of foreign-visitor sales. Kream: luxury is one of the expanding non-sneaker categories. |

### 3. Cross-border problems

| Problem | Evidence | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| Condition grades and translation | ジャンク means the seller disclaims all function; Japanese 美品 is stricter than Western "excellent"; translation apps blur this. I could not open Mercari's official 6-grade definitions (the help page returned the wrong content). | Opened |
| Returns | Proxies are bidders and forwarders, not guarantors. Auction culture is "no claims, no returns". Returns are only realistic for damage reported within about 7 days, wrong items, or "significantly not as described". The buyer bears international return shipping and proxy fees. | Opened |
| Fees | Proxy fee, domestic shipping, consolidation and international shipping, plus duty. One complaint says a ¥1,500 Mercari item became ¥4,500. | Opened and snippet |
| Authenticity | Complaints about a fake Louis Vuitton bag and counterfeit items where Buyee refused claims. | Opened |
| Shipping damage | Covered only if insurance was bought. Complaints say insurance claims were refused. | Opened |
| Customs | Undervalued declarations risk seizure with no recourse. Mercari's Zonos tie-in is a direct fix. | Opened |
