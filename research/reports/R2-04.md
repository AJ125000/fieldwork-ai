## 2. How deals work
- **Discovery:** people post in a group or chat ("Post it"), or browse Facebook Marketplace or a Facebook group. In Vietnam, shopping-group comments are used as leads. Instagram thrift shops act as storefronts (snippet).
- **Negotiation:** ad hoc, in chat. One seller in a Qatar student WhatsApp group (400+ members) deliberately lists no price (opened): https://qviews.qatar.northwestern.edu/12804/features/reuse-recycle-repurpose-how-a-whatsapp-group-chat-is-redefining-second-hand-culture-in-education-city
- **Payment by country** (from the Jakpat and Philippines/Vietnam sources above):

| Country | Methods |
|---|---|
| Indonesia | Bank transfer, COD, QRIS, convenience stores; "rekber" escrow middlemen |
| Philippines | GCash, Maya |
| Vietnam | Bank transfer; COD advised |
| India | UPI payment link sent in chat (snippet) |

- **Handover:** in-person meetup (Facebook Marketplace promotes this; Vulcan Post, an old piece) or courier.
- **Protection:** usually none.
  - Facebook Marketplace has no built-in checkout or ratings (Vulcan Post, opened, dated): https://vulcanpost.com/589732/facebook-marketplace-vs-carousell
  - Indonesian "rekber" (joint-account) middlemen are themselves informal and sometimes abscond (snippet).
  - Third-party escrow links, such as iPaymu payment links for Facebook sellers, exist (opened): https://inet.detik.com/business/d-4978346/ipaymu-rilis-solusi-pembayaran-di-facebook-marketplace
