# R1-08 · Published polls and surveys

## Brief given to the researcher

Research for a hackathon team (today is 26 Sep 2026). Find PUBLISHED consumer polls and surveys, with real sample sizes, about problems in second-hand / used-goods / online-marketplace buying and selling in JAPAC. Priority topics: item-not-as-described and disputes, returns and refunds, refund abuse by buyers, trust, off-platform payment scams, and satisfaction with platform support or dispute resolution.

Sources to check:
- India: LocalCircles (runs large national polls on e-commerce fraud, returns, counterfeits and refunds), and Consumer Affairs / National Consumer Helpline data on e-commerce complaints.
- Singapore: Milieu Insight, Blackbox Research, YouGov SG, CASE (Consumers Association of Singapore) complaint statistics on e-commerce and second-hand, ScamShield / MHA surveys.
- Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam: Rakuten Insight, YouGov, Populix (Indonesia), Jakpat (Indonesia), Q&Me (Vietnam), DTI (Philippines) consumer complaint data.
- Australia: ACCC, CHOICE, Finder surveys. Japan: National Consumer Affairs Center (Kokusen) on flea-market apps. Korea: Korea Consumer Agency on secondhand platforms.
- Company surveys: Carousell, OLX, Mercari, Vinted, eBay, OfferUp, ThredUp, Back Market, Cashify.
- Global: Global Anti-Scam Alliance (GASA) State of Scams reports for Asia, Sift/Signifyd/Riskified reports on return and refund abuse, NRF/Appriss return fraud (as global context only).

For each item: the question asked (or the finding), the result as a number, sample size, country, year, publisher and URL. Open the page and don't rely on snippets; mark any snippet-only item. Limit yourself to about 20 fetches.

Deliver (under 900 words): a table of findings sorted by relevance to disputes, refunds and scams in second-hand; then a list of survey QUESTIONS these polls used that we could reuse or adapt; then gaps where no poll exists.

## Reusable/adaptable survey questions (verbatim or near-verbatim from sources above)

1. "Have you been scammed when trying to sell your belongings (e.g. on Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree, eBay etc) in the last 12 months?" — Finder AU
2. "In the last 6/12 months, did you receive a counterfeit or fake product from an e-commerce/marketplace site?" — LocalCircles
3. "If you received a fake/wrong product, were you able to get a replacement or refund? What happened instead?" — LocalCircles
4. "Did you find a clear way to report/flag a counterfeit or misrepresented item on the platform?" — LocalCircles
5. "How often have you experienced [bait-and-switch / forced shipment after cancelling / undisclosed fees at checkout / unwanted items added to cart] when buying online?" (dark-patterns battery) — LocalCircles
6. "Which of the following scams have you experienced in the past year?" (with "buying/selling" as one category, alongside investment, phishing, etc.) — Milieu Insight
7. "How much has trust in the seller or platform influenced your decision to purchase?" — Milieu Insight
8. "Has fear of being scammed caused you to reduce your online shopping?" — Milieu Insight
9. "Have you used a flea-market/secondhand app? If so, did you experience any trouble (item not as described, late shipment, damaged item, no payment)?" — Nihon Houki Jōhō (Japan)
10. "Would a bad return/refund experience make you stop buying from a seller or platform? Would a good one make you buy more?" — Signifyd (adapt to secondhand context)

## Gaps (no published poll found)

- No dedicated consumer *survey* (as opposed to complaint/crime statistics) exists for the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, or Thailand specifically on secondhand-goods disputes/refund abuse — only aggregate SEA-wide (Milieu, GASA) or general e-commerce-behavior studies.
- No platform-published (Carousell/OLX/Mercari) survey with disclosed sample size on buyer-seller dispute satisfaction or refund-abuse-by-buyers specifically; Mercari's dispute-resolution stats could not be traced to a primary source.
- Off-platform payment scams: no country in JAPAC has a survey isolating "seller asked to pay outside platform" as its own question — this is inferred only from scam-vector breakdowns (e.g., Singapore's Facebook-linked scams, GASA's "bank transfer/e-wallet" payment channels).
- Refund abuse *by buyers* (as opposed to abuse *of* buyers) has essentially no JAPAC consumer-side survey; the only data (Sift/Signifyd) is US/global merchant-reported and not broken out for APAC.
- Satisfaction with platform dispute-resolution/support is measured indirectly (MHA ratings, Korea Consumer Agency claim volumes) but no direct "how satisfied were you with the platform's resolution of your dispute" polling question was found published with an n.

This is a genuine gap a hackathon team could fill: a short (n=500–1,000) JAPAC-wide poll on secondhand-marketplace dispute experience and resolution satisfaction, reusing/adapting the questions above, would be novel.
