## Observations on common weaknesses

1. **No listing-time baseline.** Every platform judges "not as described" only against the buyer's post-hoc claim vs. the original listing text/photos — none require or reference a tamper-evident condition record (e.g., seller-side unboxing video, verified listing photos) captured before shipment, so it's word-vs-word after the fact.
2. **Evidence is gathered reactively, not proactively.** Tracking/photo evidence is requested only once a dispute is opened (eBay, Vinted, Depop); no platform mandates buyer-side unboxing video as a standing requirement, which is the single piece of evidence that would most resolve "item swapped/damaged" disputes.
3. **Default-to-buyer via timeout, not adjudication.** Several systems (Vinted's 2-day auto-release, Bunjang's 7-day auto-confirm, Mercari's 9-day auto-complete) resolve the *absence* of a claim in the seller's favor and the *presence* of an unanswered claim in the buyer's favor (Depop: seller silence for 48h = automatic buyer refund) — i.e., silence, not evidence, decides outcomes.
4. **Escrow ≠ guaranteed protection.** Karrot and Bunjang both hold funds in "safe pay" escrow, yet Korean press coverage (Daum Jul-2026, 뉴스저널리즘) reports users being unable to get the customer center to actually release/refund held money — escrow custody and dispute *resolution* are decoupled in practice.
5. **Shrinking human-support channels compound dispute risk.** Both Mercari (chat suspended Feb 2026) and Bunjang (phone line cut) have moved dispute intake to async in-app forms/boards only, right as their escrow schemes cover 100% of transactions — support capacity doesn't appear to scale with mandatory-escrow volume.
6. **"Final" decisions with only nominal appeal.** Shopee's ToS states its guarantee decision "including any appeals" is final; Depop explicitly disclaims its protection as discretionary, not a guarantee — appeal exists nominally but the platform is both party and judge.
7. **Coverage gaps by geography and channel.** Facebook Purchase Protection is absent in India and restricted to in-app checkout (excludes the common local-pickup/Messenger flow most Marketplace deals actually use); Depop Protection is UK/US/AU-only; OLX India has no protection scheme at all — the "C2C protection" pattern is a Global-North checkout feature, not a universal safety net.
8. **Value/category carve-outs shrink real coverage.** eBay and Facebook both exclude high-risk/high-value categories (vehicles, precious metals, real estate, items ≥$2000) precisely where fraud/counterfeit risk and dispute stakes are highest.
