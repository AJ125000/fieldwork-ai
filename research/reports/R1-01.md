## 3. Economics — what works vs. fails

**Working:** (1) Ad-monetized hyperlocal C2C — Karrot profits from advertising layered on free listings, not transaction take-rate. (2) Asset-light classifieds (OLX India, Carousell core) stay profitable even while adjacent inventory-heavy units struggle. (3) Refurbisher marketplaces with inspection/warranty are reaching breakeven after long runways — Back Market hit global EBITDA breakeven only in 2025 (raised since 2014); Cars24 took until Q4 FY26. (4) Authenticated luxury consignment (The RealReal) turned profitable in 2025 after ~13 years of losses by tightening logistics/authentication costs. (5) Vinted shows a mature C2C platform can choose to trade profit for growth (logistics/payments build-out, US entry) rather than being forced to.

**Failing/struggling:** (1) Inventory-owning, reconditioning-heavy used-car models remain loss-making at scale in India — Cars24 (-₹543cr FY25) and Spinny (-₹424cr FY25) still lose money despite revenue growth; unorganized dealers hold 70% of India's used-car market. (2) Vertically-integrated C2B auto-buying was abandoned by OLX India (closed OLX Autos sales division, 2023), while the pure classifieds business it kept remains profitable — evidence that inventory ownership, not classifieds, is the hard part. (3) Standalone luxury resale brands got absorbed rather than surviving independently — Tradesy was folded into Vestiaire Collective (2022) and its app sunset by 2024. (4) Refurbished-phone category growth itself is decelerating (+3% YoY H1 2025 globally per Counterpoint) — even the most "obvious" recommerce category is not guaranteed to compound.
