Reading: quality and condition uncertainty, then fraud or trust, dominate. Hygiene matters most in clothing. Price is the pull factor, not a barrier. Value for money is the top reason to buy (Carousell regional 68%, Malaysia 78%, Philippines 92% [S for the Philippines]; Korea 56.7%; Gumtree 2016 62%).
