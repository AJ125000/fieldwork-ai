## 2. Startups / open-source / hackathon
- **FraudLens AI** — multimodal scam-listing detector (Vision Forensics MCP server + text pattern analysis), built for Hugging Face's "MCP 1st Birthday" hackathon (huggingface.co/blog/MCP-1st-Birthday/fraudlensai).
- **secondhand-mcp** (GitHub, jlsookiki) — open-source MCP server letting any MCP client search FB Marketplace/eBay/Depop/Poshmark filtered by price/condition/size/color.
- **Resell Pro / LowlandData Resale** — MCP connectors serving GDPR-clean secondhand sold-comps for pricing agents (glama.ai listings).
- **Underpriced AI** — AI item scanner + pricing tool for resellers (underpricedai.com).
- **Vision Vendor** — Google Gemini API Developer Competition entry: scan item with phone camera → instant appraisal + reselling integration (ai.google.dev/competition/projects/vision-vendor).

## 3. Google's tools
- **Gemini multimodal**: demoed for visual inspection/defect scoring (severity by size/type/location) via Gemini Live API in manufacturing contexts; "Vision Vendor" hackathon entry shows the same pattern applied to resale appraisal. No Google-shipped consumer resale-grading product found.
- **Merchant Center `condition` attribute**: values are `new` / `refurbished` / `used`; refurbished must be "professionally restored... with a warranty"; required (not optional) for used/refurbished; used/refurbished items are blocked from YouTube organic and Discover surfaces (ads-only); refurbished is disallowed entirely for vehicle listings (support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324469).
- **AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol)**: announced 17 Sep 2025 with 60+ partners; chains Intent→Cart→Payment cryptographic mandates for agent-led checkout. **Confirmed: no mention anywhere of used-goods, condition attributes, or authenticity in the AP2 spec or announcement** — it's condition-agnostic. UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) similarly handles generic search-to-cart flow with no resale-specific hooks found.

## 4. Gaps (skeptical read)
1. **No portable trust/authentication passport.** Entrupy, RealReal's Vision, and Laku6 are each siloed to one platform; a small seller listing the same bag across OLX, Carousell and Facebook can't carry one AI authentication result between them.
2. **P2P sellers are locked out of grading AI.** Cashify's SmartCheck and Cars24's pricing engine only run inside their own institutional buyback flow — an individual listing directly on OLX/Facebook/Carousell has no equivalent self-serve condition-grading tool.
3. **Agentic checkout has no condition/authenticity layer.** AP2/UCP move money but say nothing about verifying condition claims or authenticity before an agent authorizes payment — a real gap as agentic shopping grows.
4. **JAPAC coverage is narrow and category-limited.** Carousell's Certified only covers bags/sneakers/phones/cars in Singapore; India's OLX/Quikr-style P2P long tail (furniture, appliances, apparel) has no AI condition/authenticity layer at all.
5. **Regulation lags the C2C reality.** Australia's new Scams Prevention Framework (commenced 21 Feb 2025) targets banks/telcos/"digital platforms," but per Dec 2025 Treasury consultation reporting, **marketplaces and dating apps were excluded** from the initial digital-platform designation — the most scam-exposed resale channels sit outside the newest scam law. India has no resale-specific fraud rule beyond general e-commerce consumer-protection rules. Singapore's Transaction Safety Ratings is the most concrete JAPAC instrument directly rating marketplaces on anti-scam measures, but it's a disclosure scorecard, not an AI mandate.
6. **No machine-verifiable link between e-waste/refurbisher compliance and listings.** India's E-Waste Rules require CPCB registration for refurbishers, but that registered status isn't surfaced or checked at the point of listing on any marketplace reviewed.

## 5. JAPAC regulation
- **India**: E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022, tightened 2024-25 — refurbishers/recyclers must register on the CPCB EPR portal; producers must meet rising EPR collection/recovery targets (≥60% by weight from 2025, rising yearly); only CPCB-registered entities may legally refurbish/recycle.
- **Singapore**: Online Criminal Harms Act passed 5 Jul 2023 (takedown/blocking powers over scam content); separately, the Inter-Ministry Committee on Scams' **E-Commerce Marketplace Transaction Safety Ratings** (since May 2022, annual editions incl. 2024/2025) scores marketplaces on seller verification, fraud monitoring, secure payment, and dispute resolution.
- **Australia**: Scams Prevention Framework Act, royal assent/commenced 21 Feb 2025; obligates banks, telcos, and ACCC-regulated "digital platforms" on govern/prevent/detect/disrupt/respond/report; sector designations phase in from 1 Jul 2026; reportedly excludes marketplaces/dating apps from the initial digital-platform scope (per Treasury's Dec 2025 consultation, as summarized by GASA — not independently fetched).
